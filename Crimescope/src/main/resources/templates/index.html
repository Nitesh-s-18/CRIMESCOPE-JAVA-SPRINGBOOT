💡 HOW TO READ THIS CHART 24-Hour Crime Activity Profile

What it shows: Recorded crime counts broken down across every hour of the 24-hour cycle (12 AM to 11 PM).
What Day is Displayed: When viewing All Records or a Date Range, this chart aggregates all days in that period to show general time-of-day risk. When a Single Day is selected, it displays the exact 24-hour profile for that specific calendar date.
Color Legend: Red bars = Peak danger windows (Top 25%); Amber = Moderate; Sky blue = Low baseline.
Operational Action: Intensify patrol beats during evening/night danger windows (6 PM to 10 PM).

How to read: Longer horizontal bars indicate higher crime concentrations; numbers at bar ends show exact total incidents.

💡 HOW TO READ THIS HEATMAP Weekly Spatio-Temporal Matrix (Day of Week vs. Hour)

What it shows: 7-day x 24-hour heat grid displaying incident density across all 168 hours of the week.
Color Meaning: Dark Blue = Low activity (0–5 crimes); Blue/Cyan = Moderate; Amber = Elevated; Bright Red = Severe peak danger hotspot.
Operational Action: Synchronize officer shift rotations and high-intensity roving patrols during red and gold time slots.

💡 HOW TO READ THIS TIMELINE Multi-Period Longitudinal Trend Analysis

What it shows: Continuous incident volume over time with moving average baseline trend smoothing.
How to read: Spikes indicate surge periods; downward trends show positive outcomes from targeted enforcement operations.
Interactive Drill-down: Click any data point directly on the graph to filter the entire dashboard to that exact date or month.

💡 HOW TO READ THIS CHART Environmental Vulnerability (Location vs Crime Category)

What it shows: Stacked distribution revealing which crime types occur in each physical setting (Commercial, Residential, Transit, etc.).
How to read: Total bar height indicates overall environmental vulnerability; colored segments break down specific offense types.
Operational Action: Tailor situational crime prevention (e.g. enhanced streetlights and CCTV at transit hubs; neighborhood watch in residential sectors).

💡 HOW TO READ THIS CHART Full Municipal Area Incident Ranking

What it shows: Comprehensive ranked comparison of municipal sectors across Coimbatore.
How to read: Longest bars identify top-priority zones with highest cumulative crime counts.
Operational Action: Guides beat resource allocations, checkpoint stationing, and mobile squad routing.

💡 HOW TO READ THIS BOX PLOT Victim Age Demographics by Crime Category

What it shows: Distribution of victim ages across each crime category.
How to read: The horizontal line inside each box is the Median Age. The colored box spans the middle 50% of victims (25th to 75th percentiles). Whiskers extend to minimum and maximum victim ages.
Operational Action: Design tailored community safety advisories focused on the age groups most vulnerable to specific offenses.
